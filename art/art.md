# Art

## Tokens
A high-quality printable sprite sheet of round board game tokens on a pure white background. Flat vector illustration style, clean design, 1920s mafia noir aesthetic. The sheet contains a neat grid of multiple tokens of four distinct types: a classic martini glass with an olive, a brass bullet cartridge, a wooden cargo crate, and a stack of green cash bills. The tokens have thick dark borders and are evenly spaced for easy cutting with scissors.

## Rub pro Události (Events)
Události představují to, co se zrovna děje v ulicích (příjezdy vlaků, zásilky zbraní). Tematicky se sem perfektně hodí motiv večerních novin pohozených na ulici.

Prompt: A high-quality printable board game card back illustration, vertical aspect ratio. 1920s noir mafia aesthetic. A stack of vintage newspapers scattered on a wet cobblestone street at night. Rain reflecting in street puddles, atmospheric warm gas lamp lighting in the background. Centered and symmetrical composition, moody, dark, highly detailed. No readable text on the newspapers, just visual texture.

## Rub pro Herní cíle (Goals)
Cíle představují mafiánský byznys (výpalné, pašování, úplatky). Vizuál by měl evokovat zisk a plánování.

Prompt: A high-quality printable board game card back illustration, vertical aspect ratio. 1920s noir mafia aesthetic. Top-down view of a dark mahogany boss's desk. In the center is an open black leather briefcase filled with neat stacks of green 1920s cash and an elegant silver pocket watch. A wisp of cigar smoke in the air. Symmetrical composition, moody dramatic lighting, highly detailed. No text.
