# Art

## Tokens
A high-quality printable sprite sheet of round board game tokens on a pure white background. Flat vector illustration style, clean design, 1920s mafia noir aesthetic. The sheet contains a neat grid of multiple tokens of four distinct types: a classic martini glass with an olive, a brass bullet cartridge, a wooden cargo crate, and a stack of green cash bills. The tokens have thick dark borders and are evenly spaced for easy cutting with scissors.
